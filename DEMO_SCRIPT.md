# 3-minute demo script

1. Open the landing page: “StockSense helps a retail or wholesale owner move from daily stock entry to actionable buying decisions.”
2. Click **Load sample demo data**. Point out that the records are explicitly sample data.
3. Open **Data Entry** and save one “Stock received” movement. Mention the reference field and audit log make it traceable.
4. Open **Analysis**, choose the last 30 days, and show the four headline cards, sales trend, top sellers, slow movers, and top customers.
5. Open **Reorder desk**: “This is not a black box. It uses actual sales in the selected range and target cover days. With no sales, it says insufficient data rather than recommending stock.”
6. Add Nebius credentials, then ask the AI assistant: “What should I reorder and why?” Show its data-provenance line.
7. Close with: “We deliberately skipped hardware for day one. A barcode/RFID adapter only needs to call the same safe transaction interface.”
